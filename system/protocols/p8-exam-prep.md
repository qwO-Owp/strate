# P8 — Exam preparation

Strate protocol doc. When you follow it, quote its seal (last line) in the
"Lu" line of your handshake.

> **Essentials.** At exams there is no AI, no internet and no phone:
> Eliott must be independent of Strate. Until TBD-2 is decided, exam
> preparation runs at full Piscine rigor: no code, Socratic questions,
> timed blank-page drills, reconstruction from memory. Nothing, ever,
> during a real exam.

- **Used by:** the Examinateur (`[exam]`).
- **Status:** placeholder. TBD-2 is decided at the latest when push_swap
  starts (see `living/open_questions.md`).

## 1. Known so far (to confirm against official sources)

- Unlimited attempts.
- 100% is needed to move on to the next milestone. Pace deadlines apply.
- The format on Eliott's track may differ from the classic exams:
  verify on the intranet before planning.

## 2. Interim rules

- **No code from Strate.** Questions, hints about the method, and
  corrections of *his* attempts after the time is up.
- **Blank-page drills:**
  - a short exercise;
  - a timer;
  - only `man` and his memory;
  - then the correction.
- **Reconstruction:** rebuild one of his own validated functions from
  memory, then compare it with the original.
- **Edge-case interrogation:** "what if the input is empty, `NULL`, huge,
  negative?"
- **Simulators** are fine for practice. Collections of solutions are
  opened only after his own blank-page attempt, never as a study source
  before it.

## 3. To decide (TBD-2)

- the trigger: the tag only, the tag plus N days before an exam, or a
  standing weekly drill;
- the scope during an exam window: what other roles stop doing;
- the "ready" criterion;
- the post-exam debrief, recorded in `cursus_progress`.
